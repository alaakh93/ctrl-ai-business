# Claude can't hear. This gives it ears.


Claude can edit videos and make motion graphics now, but there's one thing it still can't do. It can't hear.

So every sound effect it adds is a guess. A whoosh that lands late, a click that sounds cheap, music that swells in the wrong place, and Claude has no idea.

Think of it like a DJ wearing earplugs.

So someone built Earshot. It's free and open source, and it gives Claude Code ears.

Here's how it works. Earshot listens to your video and writes down what it hears, second by second, including whether each sound actually lands on the cut. So Claude can read your soundtrack the same way it reads code.

Then it picks the right sound from its own library of two hundred and fifty-two effects, and lines it up with the exact moment.

And here's the crazy part. If nothing fits, it makes a brand new sound in code, listens to it, and keeps improving it until it sounds right.

It can even score your whole video against professional launch films, and tell you what to fix first.
