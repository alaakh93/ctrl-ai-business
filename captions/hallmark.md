If your website was made with AI, people can tell.
The same purple gradient, the same three boxes, the same "supercharge your workflow".

Hallmark is a free design skill for Claude Code, built by the team at Together AI.
It deals you three themes from a library of fifty.
It builds the page around the one thing your visitor came to do.
It checks its own work in a real browser against the tells that make a page look machine-made.
And if you already have a site, "hallmark redesign" rebuilds it and keeps your content.

Think of it like a designer who has already seen every template and refuses to hand you another one.

No more purple gradients.
No more three identical boxes.
No more guessing why your page feels generic.

(The "AI-made" page at the start is a generic example, not a real company's site. Theme pages: Hallmark's own screenshots, github.com/Nutlope/hallmark, MIT.)

Hallmark: github.com/nutlope/hallmark (MIT). Install: `npx skills add nutlope/hallmark -g --agent claude-code` (Node 18+). New page: `/hallmark a booking page for <your business>, <what visitors should do>`. Existing site: `/hallmark redesign ./your-site` (keeps your content), or `/hallmark audit ./index.html` for a report only.
(The browser checks also need `npm i -g puppeteer-core` and Chrome; without them the static checks still run. Verify the commands the day it goes out.)
